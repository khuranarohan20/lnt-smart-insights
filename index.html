First-week reach is slipping

Leads not reached in week 1 rose from 28.4% in 2026-04 to 43.7% in 2026-09, with 2804 latest-month leads.

Dial success is flat across later attempts: dial 3 connected 13.6% of 3226 leads, while dial 6 connected 8.3% of 1451 leads. Unreached leads are stopping before enough attempts. Later dials still connect, so the gap is rep follow-up rather than dial effectiveness.

First-month unreached share: 28.4Latest-month unreached share: 43.7 (n=2804)Dial 3 connection rate: 13.6 (n=3226)Dial 6 connection rate: 8.3 (n=1451)Additional buyers reached: 372

Action: Dial every unreached lead 6 times in week 1.

Impact: 372 more buyers reached in the latest month.

Caveats: Reached means a connected call with talk time in the first 7 days. Leads later marked invalid are excluded. The extra reach assumes additional dials connect at recent rates for the same attempt number.

Objections are ending calls

Offering an alternative went with 7.8% site visits agreed among 474 objection leads versus 4% among 1416 without one; alternatives were offered on 25.1%.

When buyers raised objections, reps offered an alternative L&T project in only 25.1% of 1890 objection leads. Price and location objections show the same pattern: 8.4% versus 4.2% for price, and 5.1% versus 0.8% for location. Offering a nearby matching project keeps the conversation open when the original option does not fit.

Alternative offered rate: 25.1 (n=1890)With alternative agreed rate: 7.8 (n=474)Without alternative agreed rate: 4 (n=1416)Price with alternative rate: 8.4 (n=227)Price without alternative rate: 4.2 (n=571)Location with alternative rate: 5.1 (n=177)Location without alternative rate: 0.8 (n=391)Additional site visits agreed: 54Visit lift: 58.1 (n=93)

Action: On every objection, pitch the nearest matching L&T project before the call ends.

Impact: 54 more site visits agreed, a 58.1% lift on 93 site visits agreed among objection leads.

Caveats: Objections and site visits agreed are LLM judgments on about 50% of analysed calls. This is an association, not proven cause. Offering an alternative also goes with longer, more engaged calls.

Probing lifts hot-call visits

Hot buyers agreed to a site visit at 51.2% after probing versus 33.8% without probing, across 203 and 207 leads.

The gap holds across every month and across short, medium and long calls. Probing means asking about budget, configuration and move-in timeline before proposing the visit. The pattern is not explained by longer calls: it appears in short, medium and long calls alike.

Probed agreed rate: 51.2 (n=203)Not-probed agreed rate: 33.8 (n=207)Probed leads: 203Not-probed leads: 207Additional site visits agreed: 36Visit lift: 20.7 (n=174)

Action: On every hot call, ask about budget, configuration and move-in timeline before proposing the visit, and coach reps on it.

Impact: 36 more site visits agreed, a 20.7% lift on 174 site visits agreed among hot leads.

Caveats: Probing and site visits agreed are AI scores on the same call; part of the gap may reflect buyer engagement. Hot is itself an AI label. One call per lead was rated.

Wrong-fit objections waste calls

46.7% of objection buyers were wrong-fit: 882 of 1890. They agreed to a site visit at 2.5% versus 7% for 1008 other objection buyers.

Wrong-fit buyers wanted a different area, a specific suburb or were far over budget. Their site-visit agreement rate was lower in every month. Location and starting price are filtering issues that can be addressed before sales calls buyers.

Wrong-fit share: 46.7 (n=1890)Wrong-fit leads: 882Objection leads: 1890Wrong-fit agreed rate: 2.5 (n=882)Right-fit agreed rate: 7 (n=1008)Right-fit leads: 1008Additional site visits agreed: 40

Action: Put the location and starting price in every ad and lead form.

Impact: 40 more site visits agreed if these leads had been right-fit buyers.

Caveats: This covers buyers who raised an objection on an analysed call, not all leads. Objections and site visits agreed are LLM judgments on about 50% of analysed calls. This is an association, not proven cause.
